Stuart Delivery Tax: UK Guide 2026

Are Stuart Couriers Self-Employed?

Yes. Stuart classifies all delivery partners as self-employed independent contractors. Stuart pays you gross — no tax is deducted at source. You must register for Self-Assessment and pay Income Tax and Class 4 NI directly to HMRC.

Allowable Expenses for Stuart Couriers

  • Mileage at HMRC approved rates (45p/mile car, 20p/mile bicycle)
  • Smartphone — percentage used for Stuart app and navigation
  • Thermal delivery bags and equipment
  • Bicycle or vehicle insurance (courier-specific policy)
  • Helmet, hi-vis jacket, gloves (safety equipment)
  • Bicycle maintenance and repairs
  • Accountant fees for Self-Assessment

Self-Assessment Deadlines

  • 5 October 2026 — register for Self-Assessment (new starters)
  • 31 January 2027 — online return deadline for 2025–26
  • 31 January 2027 — pay outstanding tax
