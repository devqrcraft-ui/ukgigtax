About UKGigTax

A free, independent tax reference for delivery riders, couriers, drivers and other self-employed gig workers in the UK.

What this site is

UKGigTax builds free calculators and plain-English guides for people who earn money through platforms such as Deliveroo, Uber Eats, Amazon Flex, Just Eat, Bolt Food, Stuart and Wolt. Every tool runs in your browser and needs no signup. We are an independent reference site: we are not HMRC, we are not a delivery platform, and we are not affiliated with either.

Editorial standards

Who writes and checks the content

Pages are written and maintained by the UKGigTax editorial team. We do not publish invented author profiles, qualifications, client counts or testimonials. If a page says a rate or threshold, it is there because it can be traced to official guidance.

We make money from advertising and, where disclosed, affiliate links. Advertising does not influence which calculators we build or what the figures say. No platform or accountant can pay to change a rate, a ranking or a recommendation.

Method

How we check the numbers

Tax rates and thresholds are checked against GOV.UK before they are published. The calculators use the 2026/27 Personal Allowance of £12,570, Class 4 National Insurance at 6% between £12,570 and £50,270 and 2% above that, and HMRC mileage rates of 55p for the first 10,000 car or van miles, 25p after that, 24p for motorcycles and 20p for bicycles.

Class 2 is treated as paid when profit is at least £7,105 a year; below that it is voluntary at £3.65 a week. The VAT registration threshold is £90,000 of rolling 12-month turnover. Scottish income tax bands are handled separately from the rest of the UK.

Every calculator page shows when it was last reviewed. The current full review date is 27 October 2026. If GOV.UK changes a rate, the page is updated rather than left with an old figure.

Limits

What the calculators do not do

The results are estimates, not tax advice. They assume your only income is the figures you enter, that you are resident in the UK for tax purposes, and that your expenses are allowable. They do not cover every relief, benefit, student loan plan, pension contribution or complex self-assessment position.

Your actual liability can differ. For a decision that matters — a large bill, a VAT question, an investigation or a cross-border situation — check GOV.UK and speak to a qualified accountant.

Privacy

What happens to the figures you enter

The calculators run locally in your browser. Your income, expenses and mileage are not sent to us or stored on a server. Some pages put the values you type into the page URL so you can share or bookmark a result; that URL stays in your browser unless you choose to share it.

We use privacy-friendly analytics to count page views. Read the privacy policy and terms of use for the full detail.
