Privacy — short and specific

Privacy Policy

What UKGigTax does and does not collect. Last updated 27 October 2026.

The short version

UKGigTax has no accounts, no login and no server-side storage of your figures. Calculator inputs are processed in your browser. We use Google Analytics 4 with IP anonymisation to count page views. Nothing you type into a calculator is sent to us.

Calculator data

1. What happens to the figures you enter

Incomes, expenses, mileage, hours and similar inputs stay on your device. They are not uploaded, not saved to an account and not linked to you. Some pages write the values you entered into the page address (URL) so a result can be bookmarked or shared. That address stays in your browser unless you choose to copy it. If you share a result link, the figures in it become visible to whoever opens it.

Analytics

2. Cookies and measurement

We use Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-8TRDK9LTCG) to see which pages are visited, how the site is found and which calculators are used. IP addresses are anonymised in the configuration, and we do not send the values you type into a calculator to analytics. Google may set cookies for this measurement. You can block or delete them in your browser settings, and the calculators will keep working.

Advertising or affiliate links, where present, may be served by third parties that set their own cookies under their own policies.

Email

3. If you contact us

If you email hello@ukgigtax.com, we use your address and message only to reply and to fix the issue you raised. We do not add you to a mailing list and do not sell or share your address.

Third parties

4. Links and hosting

Pages link to GOV.UK and sometimes to other resources. Those sites have their own privacy policies, which we do not control. The site is served by Cloudflare Pages, which processes standard request data such as IP address and user agent to deliver pages and protect the site from abuse. Google Analytics and Cloudflare operate globally and may process request data outside the UK under their own published transfer safeguards.

Security

5. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Because calculator inputs never leave your browser, there is no stored tax data on our side to expose. Email is not end-to-end encrypted, so do not send documents or sensitive personal details you would not want sitting in an inbox.

Children

6. Children

The calculators are intended for people earning income in the UK and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your rights

7. Access, correction and deletion

Because we do not hold calculator inputs or user accounts, there is usually no personal record to access or delete. For analytics, email or hosting data you can contact hello@ukgigtax.com and we will help within the limits of what we hold. If you are in the UK, you also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

Changes

8. Updates to this policy

If what we collect changes, this page will be updated first. The date at the top shows the last revision. See also our terms of service.
