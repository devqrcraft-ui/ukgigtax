Deliveroo Driver Tax Guide: London

Operating as a Deliveroo rider in London means you are self-employed in the eyes of HMRC. This guide covers everything you need to know about staying tax-compliant while working in the capital.

HMRC Registration

If you earn more than £1,000 in a tax year, you must register for Self Assessment:

  • Deadline: Register by 5 October following the end of the tax year.
  • UTR Number: You will receive a Unique Taxpayer Reference.

London-Specific Expenses

You can deduct business costs to reduce your tax bill:

  • Congestion Charge: Deductible if incurred during deliveries.
  • Equipment: Thermal bags, waterproof gear.
  • Mileage: 45p/mile for cars or 20p/mile for bicycles.
