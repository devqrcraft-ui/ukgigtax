Deliveroo Tax Guide — London 2026

By Ethan Blake · Tax Compliance Specialist · Updated June 2026 · ~5 min read
2026 Tax Summary — London

Deliveroo workers in London pay 6% Class 4 NI on profits over £12,570 in 2025/26. On £28 000 gross: approximately £926 NI + £2 160 Income Tax = £3 086 total. Mileage: 45p/mile (first 10,000). Self Assessment deadline: 31 January 2027. London riders often use bicycles — claim 20p/mile for all cycling.

Key Takeaways
  • Class 4 NI: 6% on profits £12,570–£50,270, then 2% above
  • Mileage allowance: 45p/mile (first 10,000 miles), 25p/mile after
  • Personal Allowance: £12,570 — no tax on income below this
  • Register for Self Assessment by 5 October 2026 if first year
  • 1099-NEC equivalent: HMRC Digital Platform Reporting — platforms report your earnings directly

Frequently Asked Questions

How much tax does a Deliveroo rider pay in London?
A Deliveroo rider in London earning £28,000 pays approximately £926 Class 4 NI + £2,160 Income Tax = £3,086 total. Mileage deductions significantly reduce this.
Can London Deliveroo cyclists claim mileage?
Yes. Bicycle riders claim 20p per mile under HMRC AMAP. Car/motorbike riders claim 45p/mile for first 10,000 miles.
Does Deliveroo report earnings to HMRC?
Yes. Under Digital Platform Reporting rules since January 2024, Deliveroo reports all UK driver earnings to HMRC annually.
What is the Self Assessment deadline for Deliveroo riders?
Online Self Assessment deadline is 31 January 2027 for the 2025/26 tax year. Register by 5 October 2026 if first year.
Can I claim the Trading Allowance as a Deliveroo rider?
Yes, if gross income is under £1,000 you pay no tax. Above that, you can still use the £1,000 Trading Allowance instead of itemising expenses.
