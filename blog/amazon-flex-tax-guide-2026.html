Amazon Flex Tax Guide 2026 UK: How Much Tax Do Drivers Pay?

Last updated: May 2026 · By Ethan Blake · Tax Compliance Specialist · ~9 min read · 2,200 words

Amazon Flex drivers in the UK are self-employed. Amazon does not deduct any tax from your payments. On a profit of £30,000, you owe approximately £4,527 in total tax: £3,486 Income Tax and £1,041 Class 4 National Insurance. You must register with HMRC and submit a Self Assessment return by 31 January 2027 for the 2025/26 tax year.

Key Takeaways

  • Amazon Flex pays you gross — no tax is deducted at source
  • Register for Self Assessment by 5 October 2026 if you are new to self-employment
  • Mileage allowance: 45p per mile for first 10,000 business miles, 25p thereafter
  • Class 4 NI rate: 6% on profits between £12,570 and £50,270 (Class 2 abolished April 2024)
  • Trading allowance: first £1,000 of self-employment income is tax-free
  • Online Self Assessment deadline: 31 January 2027 for the 2025/26 tax year

Is Amazon Flex Classed as Self-Employment in the UK?

Yes. Amazon Flex drivers are classed as self-employed sole traders under UK law. Amazon engages you as an independent contractor, not an employee. This means Amazon pays you a gross amount for each delivery block you complete and does not withhold Income Tax or National Insurance on your behalf.

As a self-employed worker, you are fully responsible for:

  • Registering with HMRC as self-employed
  • Tracking your income and allowable expenses throughout the year
  • Submitting an annual Self Assessment tax return
  • Paying your Income Tax and Class 4 National Insurance by the deadline

"You must register for Self Assessment and send a tax return if you are self-employed as a sole trader and earned more than £1,000."

— GOV.UK — Self Assessment tax returns

If you also hold a PAYE job, your Amazon Flex income is treated as additional self-employment income. You must declare it separately on your Self Assessment return regardless of how much you earn from employment.

How Much Tax Does an Amazon Flex Driver Pay in 2026?

Your tax bill is calculated on your taxable profit — your total Amazon Flex earnings minus your allowable expenses. The Personal Allowance for 2025/26 is £12,570.

Annual Profit Income Tax Class 4 NI (6%) Total Tax
£12,570 or less £0 £0 £0
£20,000 £1,486 £447 £1,933
£25,000 £2,486 £747 £3,233
£30,000 (typical Amazon Flex) £3,486 £1,041 £4,527
£35,000 £4,486 £1,341 £5,827
£50,270 (higher rate threshold) £7,540 £2,262 £9,802

Class 4 NI is charged at 6% on profits between £12,570 and £50,270. Class 2 National Insurance was abolished from April 2024. Use the UKGigTax calculator to estimate your exact bill based on your actual earnings and mileage.

Why Your Profit Is Lower Than Your Gross Earnings

Amazon Flex pays you a gross block rate. Before calculating your tax, you subtract all allowable expenses from your gross earnings. For a driver covering 12,000 business miles per year, the mileage allowance alone reduces taxable profit by £4,150 (10,000 miles at 45p + 2,000 miles at 25p). This can bring a £34,150 gross income down to a £30,000 taxable profit.

What Mileage Allowance Can Amazon Flex Drivers Claim?

Mileage is the single largest deduction available to Amazon Flex drivers. HMRC allows self-employed workers to claim a flat rate per business mile rather than tracking actual fuel and vehicle costs.

Vehicle Type First 10,000 Miles Above 10,000 Miles
Car or van 45p per mile 25p per mile
Motorcycle 24p per mile 24p per mile
Bicycle 20p per mile 20p per mile

Amazon Flex deliveries are carried out by car or van, so the standard 45p per mile rate applies. You must log each journey with the date, start and end points, and the business purpose. A simple spreadsheet or a mileage-tracking app is sufficient.

Important: You Cannot Switch Methods Mid-Year

Once you choose the HMRC mileage allowance method for a vehicle, you must use it for the entire life of that vehicle in your business. You cannot switch to actual costs later. Choose mileage allowance from the start — it is simpler and usually more tax-efficient for gig drivers.

If you cover 15,000 business miles in a year, your total mileage deduction is £5,750: (10,000 × 45p) + (5,000 × 25p). This is a significant reduction in your taxable profit.

What Expenses Can Amazon Flex Drivers Claim?

In addition to mileage, you can claim any expense that is incurred wholly and exclusively for your Amazon Flex work. Mixed-use expenses must be apportioned to the business element only.

  • Mobile phone: The business-use proportion of your monthly contract or pay-as-you-go costs. If you use your phone 60% for work, claim 60% of the bill
  • Equipment: Phone mounts, delivery bags, USB chargers, and any tools used solely for delivery work
  • Insurance: Hire and reward (courier) insurance, or any additional premium you pay specifically for delivery work
  • Professional fees: Accountant or tax adviser fees, and the cost of tax software subscriptions
  • Parking: Parking fees paid during a delivery (but not fines)
  • Congestion or road charges: Fees paid while making deliveries in charge zones

You cannot claim ordinary clothing, food and drink, or the cost of commuting from your home to the Amazon depot. The journey begins when you start a delivery block, not when you leave your house.

Trading Allowance vs Actual Expenses: Which Should You Use?

If your total allowable expenses are less than £1,000, you can simply claim the trading allowance instead and deduct £1,000 from your gross income without keeping expense records. However, most Amazon Flex drivers with meaningful mileage will find that their actual expenses — particularly mileage — far exceed £1,000, making detailed record-keeping worthwhile.

How Do You Submit a Self Assessment Return as an Amazon Flex Driver?

If this is your first year as self-employed, register with HMRC before 5 October 2026. You will receive your Unique Taxpayer Reference (UTR) by post within 10 working days.

  1. Gather your earnings data. Export your earnings history from the Amazon Flex app. This shows all block payments received between 6 April 2025 and 5 April 2026
  2. Total your expenses. Add up your mileage log, phone bills, equipment receipts, and any other allowable costs for the tax year
  3. Calculate your taxable profit. Subtract total expenses from gross Amazon Flex earnings
  4. Log in to HMRC online. Visit GOV.UK and sign in with your Government Gateway account
  5. Complete the SA103S supplementary pages. Enter your self-employment income and expenses on the Self-Employment short form (suitable if your turnover is below £85,000)
  6. Review the tax calculation. HMRC calculates your Income Tax and Class 4 NI automatically
  7. Submit and pay by 31 January 2027. Pay via bank transfer using your UTR as the payment reference

What Are Payments on Account and Do They Affect You?

If your Self Assessment tax bill exceeds £1,000, HMRC requires you to make payments on account — advance payments toward your next year's tax bill. This catches many new Amazon Flex drivers off guard.

  • Each payment on account is 50% of your previous year's tax bill
  • First payment on account: due 31 January 2027 (same day as your 2025/26 balance payment)
  • Second payment on account: due 31 July 2027
  • If your earnings fall significantly, you can apply to reduce your payments on account via your HMRC online account

On a £4,527 tax bill, your total payment due on 31 January 2027 would be £6,790: £4,527 owed for 2025/26 plus £2,263 as the first payment on account for 2026/27. Set aside money monthly to avoid a cash-flow shock.

What Records Must Amazon Flex Drivers Keep?

HMRC requires you to retain financial records for at least 5 years after the 31 January submission deadline. For the 2025/26 return, keep records until at least January 2032.

  • Income records: Screenshots or exports of your Amazon Flex earnings statements from the app
  • Mileage log: Date, start point, destination, and business purpose for every journey
  • Expense receipts: Receipts or invoices for all claimed expenses, including phone bills and insurance
  • Bank statements: To reconcile Amazon payments received against your records
  • Previous tax returns: Keep copies of submitted returns and HMRC correspondence

Digital records are acceptable. Use a dedicated folder in cloud storage or an accounting app to organise everything by tax year.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Amazon Flex self-employment in the UK?

Yes. Amazon Flex drivers are classed as self-employed. Amazon does not deduct Income Tax or National Insurance from your payments. You must register with HMRC and submit a Self Assessment return each year.

How much tax does an Amazon Flex driver pay in 2026?

On a profit of £30,000, you owe approximately £3,486 in Income Tax and £1,041 in Class 4 NI, totalling £4,527. The effective tax rate is roughly 15% at this income level.

Can Amazon Flex drivers claim mileage allowance?

Yes. You can claim 45p per mile for the first 10,000 business miles and 25p per mile thereafter. Keep a mileage log recording dates, destinations, and the business purpose of each trip.

Do I need to register for Self Assessment as an Amazon Flex driver?

Yes. If your Amazon Flex income exceeds £1,000 in a tax year, you must register for Self Assessment by 5 October following the end of that tax year.

What expenses can Amazon Flex drivers claim?

You can claim mileage allowance, the business proportion of mobile phone costs, delivery equipment, courier insurance, parking fees during deliveries, and professional accountancy fees.

When is the Self Assessment deadline for Amazon Flex drivers?

The online Self Assessment deadline for the 2025/26 tax year is 31 January 2027. Tax owed must also be paid by this date. Register by 5 October 2026 if you are new to self-employment.

Does Amazon Flex send a tax summary to HMRC?

Amazon provides earnings statements in the Flex app but does not report your income to HMRC directly. You are responsible for calculating your taxable profit and submitting your own return.
