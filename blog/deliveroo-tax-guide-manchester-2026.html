Deliveroo Tax Guide — Manchester 2026

By Ethan Blake · Tax Compliance Specialist · Updated June 2026 · ~5 min read
2026 Tax Summary — Manchester

Deliveroo workers in Manchester pay 6% Class 4 NI on profits over £12,570 in 2025/26. On £25 000 gross: approximately £746 NI + £1 740 Income Tax = £2 486 total. Mileage: 45p/mile (first 10,000). Self Assessment deadline: 31 January 2027.

Key Takeaways
  • Class 4 NI: 6% on profits £12,570–£50,270, then 2% above
  • Mileage allowance: 45p/mile (first 10,000 miles), 25p/mile after
  • Personal Allowance: £12,570 — no tax on income below this
  • Register for Self Assessment by 5 October 2026 if first year
  • 1099-NEC equivalent: HMRC Digital Platform Reporting — platforms report your earnings directly

Frequently Asked Questions

How much tax does a Deliveroo rider pay in Manchester?
A Deliveroo rider in Manchester earning £25,000 pays approximately £746 Class 4 NI + £1,740 Income Tax = £2,486 total after the £12,570 Personal Allowance.
Do Deliveroo riders in Manchester need to register for Self Assessment?
Yes, if gross income exceeds £1,000 in the tax year. Register online at GOV.UK by 5 October 2026 for 2025/26.
Can Manchester Deliveroo riders claim bicycle mileage?
Yes. HMRC AMAP rate for bicycles is 20p per mile. Track every business mile carefully.
Is there a Manchester-specific tax rate for gig workers?
No. UK Income Tax rates and NI rates are the same across England. The standard Personal Allowance of £12,570 and Class 4 NI rate of 6% apply.
What records should Manchester Deliveroo riders keep?
Mileage logs, bank statements showing earnings, receipts for equipment and phone, and any HMRC correspondence. Keep records for 5 years after the filing deadline.
