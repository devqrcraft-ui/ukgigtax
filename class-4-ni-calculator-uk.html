Class 2

The small profits threshold

Class 2 is £3.65 a week, or £189.80 for 52 weeks. When your self-employed profit is at least £7,105, Class 2 is treated as paid — you do not receive a separate bill. It still counts towards your State Pension and certain benefits.

If your profit is below £7,105, Class 2 is not treated as paid. You can choose to pay it voluntarily to protect your contributory benefit record.

Benefits

What Class 4 does and does not cover

Class 4 National Insurance does not count towards the State Pension or contributory benefits. Class 2 does. That is why the Class 2 rules matter even though the amount is small.

If you have gaps in your National Insurance record, check your State Pension forecast on GOV.UK before paying voluntary contributions.

Payments

Class 4 and payments on account

Class 4 NI is collected through Self Assessment. It is included in the tax bill used to calculate payments on account. If your bill is over £1,000, you may pay half by 31 January and half by 31 July.
